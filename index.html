Clause 4.3

ISMS Scope

The ISMS covers the design, development, hosting, and operation of the Northwind analytics SaaS platform — all AWS infrastructure, the corporate Google Workspace tenant, and every employee or contractor who accesses customer data or production systems.

In Scope
  • Production & staging AWS accounts across all regions in use (eu-west-2, us-east-1)
  • The analytics SaaS application — web app, API, background workers, primary data store
  • Corporate IT — Google Workspace, issued endpoint devices, the HQ office network
  • All employees, contractors & interns with access to production systems or customer data
  • Supplier relationships where the supplier processes customer data or accesses production
Out of Scope
  • Marketing website — static, no customer data, no authentication, hosted separately
  • Shared co-working space — not a Northwind-controlled facility, no persistent asset storage
  • BYOD personal devices used solely for email — covered by Acceptable Use policy and MDM email-only profile instead

Clause 6.1.2 & 6.1.3

Risk Assessment & Treatment

An asset-based methodology: Risk = Likelihood × Impact, scored 1–5 on each axis. Every risk above Low gets one of four treatments, and Critical/High risk requires documented sign-off — acceptance is never silent.

Treatment Options

MODIFY
Apply a control to reduce likelihood and/or impact — e.g. enforce MFA to cut account-takeover likelihood.
RETAIN
Knowingly accept the risk as-is, with documented, time-bound sign-off per the acceptance matrix.
AVOID
Stop the activity causing the risk — e.g. decommission a legacy integration rather than patch it indefinitely.
SHARE
Transfer risk via a third party — e.g. cyber insurance, or outsourcing card processing to a PCI DSS processor.

Clause 4–10 conformance ahead of certification

Finding Classification

Major Nonconformity
A required control is absent, or the ISMS process itself has broken down. Immediate corrective action, re-audited before the next certification milestone.
Minor Nonconformity
A control is implemented but not consistently, or evidence is incomplete. Corrective action required within 90 days.
Observation
Not yet a nonconformity, but a trend or weak point worth tracking. Logged for monitoring, no mandatory deadline.
Opportunity for Improvement
The control is effective, but a better way exists. Optional — the owner may pick it up voluntarily.

Clause 9.3

Management Review

Quarterly, chaired by the CEO. Clause 9.3 specifies mandatory inputs and outputs — a review that skips an input, or produces no recorded output, is itself a common audit finding.

Clause 10.1

Corrective Action Tracking

Every nonconformity is tracked through to closure, keeping three things distinct — a frequent point of confusion auditors specifically probe.

01 · Correction
Fix the instance
e.g. revoke one over-privileged account immediately.
02 · Root Cause
Why it happened
e.g. offboarding isn't triggered automatically from the HRIS.
03 · Corrective Action
Prevent recurrence
e.g. integrate HRIS termination events with SSO to auto-disable access.

A correction without a documented root cause and corrective action does not close a nonconformity under Clause 10.1. Four open items are currently tracked, sourced from internal audit findings, self-identification, and an incident post-mortem — each with an owner and due date, reported at every management review until closed.
